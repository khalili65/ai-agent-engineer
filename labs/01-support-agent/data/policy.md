# Nova Travel travel policy

## baggage
- Every flight includes one cabin bag of up to 8 kg.
- A checked bag of up to 23 kg costs 35 dollars each way. Add it at least 2 days before the flight.

## changes
- You can move the dates of a trip. More than 7 days before the trip, a change is free.
- 7 days or less before the trip, a change costs 30 dollars, if the hotel has free rooms.
- The name on a booking cannot be changed. Cancel the booking and book again.
- A person on our team confirms every change.

## cancellations
- To cancel a trip, write to support with your booking number.
- The refund rules decide how much money comes back.
- After a person approves a refund, the money reaches the card in 5 to 10 working days.
